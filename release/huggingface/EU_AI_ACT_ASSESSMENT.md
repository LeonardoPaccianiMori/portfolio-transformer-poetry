# EU AI Act Release-Preparation Assessment

**Assessment date:** 2026-08-21
**Status:** owner-reviewed for this bounded release; no claim of compliance

This document is a factual preparation record, not legal advice or a binding
classification.

The planned artifacts adapt the existing Minerva 7B model through a small
three-stage full-weight curriculum and a rank-8 DPO adapter. Documented
adaptation compute appears far below the European Commission guidance's
indicative substantial-modification comparison, but that guidance is
non-binding and does not itself determine whether Leonardo would be a
general-purpose AI model provider.

If provider obligations apply, Article 53 considerations may include technical
documentation, downstream information, a copyright/TDM-reservation policy, and
a sufficiently detailed public training-content summary. The open-source
exception does not remove the copyright-policy and training-summary duties,
and a non-commercial license may not satisfy every open-source condition.

Preparation therefore includes:

- `COPYRIGHT_AND_TDM_POLICY.md`;
- an artifact-specific aggregate `TRAINING_CONTENT_SUMMARY.md`;
- exact parent, selected-state, package, and file hashes;
- cumulative source-family target-token exposure;
- documented evaluation, limitations, intended purpose, and AI contribution.

For this bounded release, the owner reviewed the available assessment and chose
to publish aggregate training-content summaries, copyright/TDM policy,
lineage, limitations, and downstream loading information without claiming a
formal legal classification or compliance determination. Applicable
Commission/AI Office templates and guidance must be refreshed if the artifacts
or distribution model materially change. No claim of compliance is made.

## 2026-09-15 Addendum — synthetic teacher data

The second-generation artifact `poem_follower_distilled` was trained partly on
sonnets generated by the third-party DeepSeek Open Platform model
`deepseek-chat` on 2026-09-15. The project recorded the provider, model, date,
generated volume, filtering criteria (the project's prosody checker, a
failed-line limit, and, for repairs, preservation of the planned ending words),
and the licence basis in the terms of service, section 4.2. That section
permits using Outputs to train other models, including model distillation, and
section 4.2(2) assigns rights in the Outputs to the user. Caveats recorded:
PRC governing law; the prohibition on implying DeepSeek partnership or
endorsement; the duty to disclose AI-generated content to end users; and
export-control and sanctions compliance. No synthetic text is distributed.
