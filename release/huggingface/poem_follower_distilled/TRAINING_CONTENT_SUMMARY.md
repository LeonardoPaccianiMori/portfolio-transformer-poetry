# Aggregate Project-Adaptation Training-Content Summary — Distilled Poem-Follower Adapter

This summary is prepared for transparency and possible EU AI Act use. It is
not a claim that a particular regulatory classification applies.

It covers only this project's adaptation work. It does not reproduce or
independently verify the Minerva parent's upstream pretraining, SFT, or
preference-data summary; see the pinned parent disclosure at
<https://huggingface.co/sapienzanlp/Minerva-7B-instruct-v1.0/tree/d1fc0f0e589ae879c5ac763e0e4206a4d14a3f6d>.

Parent chain: `stage3` -> `dpo_adapter` -> `plan_following_adapter` -> `plan_follower_v2` -> `poem_follower_distilled`.

The adapter writes a poem to a supplied plan and was trained on 1,103 cards:
507 scheme-valid free-mode sonnets generated by the third-party model
`deepseek-chat`, 396 grammar-repair outputs in which that model corrected the
grammar of the project's own valid-but-ungrammatical poems while keeping the
ending words, and 200 of the project's own pipeline cards. Training ran for
268 optimizer updates (4 epochs, 1,297 seconds).

Measured on the frozen 960-plan grid with the project's plan generator, the
composed scheme validity without repair is 0.6917, the stage-two poem validity
0.7374, accepted hendecasyllable lines 7.3 of 14, and failed lines 0.93. The
same protocol without the synthetic data reached 0.6250 composed (plan
generator plus `plan_follower_v2`).

Coherence is the limit. A calibrated two-judge AI panel scored the composed
poems 2.917 and 2.725 of 5, against 2.875 and 2.750 for the pre-distillation
pipeline. The teacher sonnets themselves scored 4.12 (planned mode) and 4.47
(free mode), so the experiment shows that form transfers and coherence does
not. The checker measures metre, rhyme, scheme, and structure, not literary
quality.


## Synthetic teacher data

This adapter was trained in part on outputs of the third-party model
`deepseek-chat`, served through the DeepSeek Open Platform API operated by
Hangzhou DeepSeek Artificial Intelligence Co., Ltd. The outputs were produced
on 2026-09-15 for research adaptation and were filtered before use.

- Volume: about 4,300 generated sonnets; 507 free-mode poems and 396 grammar
  repairs entered this adapter's card set after the project's checker kept
  only scheme-valid outputs (and, for repairs, outputs that preserved the
  planned ending words).
- Basis: the DeepSeek Open Platform Terms of Service (release 2026-04-22,
  effective 2026-04-29) section 4.2 state that the user "may apply the Inputs
  and Outputs of the Services to a wide range of use cases, including personal
  use, academic research, derivative product development, training other
  models (such as model distillation), etc.", and section 4.2(2) assigns
  rights in the Outputs to the user.
- Caveats recorded by the project: the terms are governed by the laws of the
  People's Republic of China; sections 5.2-5.4 forbid implying partnership,
  endorsement, or co-creation with DeepSeek; section 8.1 requires disclosing
  AI-generated content to end users; export-control and sanctions rules apply.
- No synthetic sonnet text, prompt text, or teacher output is distributed in
  this package or in the public project repositories.
