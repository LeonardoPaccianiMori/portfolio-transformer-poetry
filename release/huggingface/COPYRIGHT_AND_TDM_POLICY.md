# Copyright and Text-and-Data-Mining Policy for the Planned Model Release

## Status and purpose

This is a release-preparation policy, not legal advice, specialist clearance,
or upload authorization. It records the project's operational approach to
copyright and text-and-data-mining reservations for the four planned model
artifacts.

## Policy

1. Preserve the exact Minerva parent identifier, revision, visible Apache-2.0
   metadata, source link, and Sapienza NLP attribution.
2. Publish aggregate source-family and target-token exposure for every selected
   artifact. Do not hide uncertain source families.
3. Do not redistribute corpus text, token streams, prompts, openings,
   preferences, votes, annotations, generations, or private mappings through
   the model repositories.
4. Retain source-specific citations, Creative Commons versions, commercial-use
   boundaries, ShareAlike notices, and archive links. Do not claim one
   repository-wide data license.
5. Treat lawful training access, corpus redistribution, and weight distribution
   as separate questions. Do not represent an Article 4 TDM analysis as a
   model-distribution license.
6. For future acquisitions or retraining, check machine-readable reservations,
   site terms, item-level licenses, database rights, and access conditions at
   acquisition time. Record evidence and exclude unresolved sources.
7. Use artifact-specific hashes, allowlists, model cards, source summaries, and
   validation. Discovery of an incompatible attachment, private artifact, or
   recalled protected text fails closed.
8. Route substantiated copyright or privacy concerns through the public GitHub
   repository's issue/contact surface. Preserve a hash-pinned remediation
   record; visibility changes cannot retract prior downloads, mirrors, or
   caches.

## Release assumption

The planned CC BY-NC 4.0 structure applies only to rights Leonardo controls. It
is usable only under the unresolved assumption that training-data licenses do
not govern the weights. If incompatible ShareAlike terms are determined to
attach, distribution is not authorized under this structure.
